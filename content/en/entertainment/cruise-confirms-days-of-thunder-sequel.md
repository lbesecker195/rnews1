# Cruise Confirms Days of Thunder Sequel

**Tom Cruise** announced a *Days of Thunder* sequel with **Anne Hathaway** in a **Saturday** video from **Daytona International Speedway**, posing beside a *lime-green* **NASCAR** car tagged for *summer 2028*. The confirmation did not arrive as a rumor dressed up as news. It arrived as the star standing at the sport's most famous oval, next to sheet metal already labeled for a summer still almost two years away, and naming a co-star whose presence immediately became the other half of the story.

**Paramount** dated the *theatrical IMAX* release for **June 2, 2028**, *nearly 40 years* after **Tony Scott**'s **1990** original. That is a studio date, not a hoped-for window. It is also a historical interval. Hollywood does not usually wait four decades to continue a stock-car drama. It waits that long when the original driver is still the original actor, the original title still means something in the culture, and a studio is willing to put a summer **IMAX** slot on the board.

The announcement lands as Cruise's *first major IP follow-up* after winding down **Mission: Impossible**, alongside *another Top Gun* and *prestige work*. Entertainment feeds already treated the combination as a guarantee of **NASCAR**-nostalgia and *casting discourse*. The facts do not require extra invention. A Daytona video, a lime-green car, a June 2, 2028 IMAX date, Hathaway as a race-team owner and engineer, and a pregnancy joke about waiting until after the baby were enough.
