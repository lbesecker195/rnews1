# PWHL and Scripps Sports Announce National U.S. Television Partnership for 2026-27 Season

## A Historic Milestone for Women's Professional Hockey

The Professional Women's Hockey League (PWHL) has announced a groundbreaking national television partnership with Scripps Sports, effective for the 2026-27 season. This agreement represents a pivotal moment for women's professional hockey in North America, signaling increased mainstream media attention and expanded accessibility for fans across the United States.

## The Significance of the Partnership

The announcement of the PWHL's partnership with Scripps Sports marks a substantial step forward in the league's mission to establish women's professional hockey as a premier sporting attraction in North America. Since its inaugural 2024-25 season, the PWHL has garnered unprecedented attention and support, breaking attendance records and generating significant viewership numbers. This new television agreement amplifies that momentum and demonstrates the league's commitment to reaching broader audiences.

Scripps Sports, a major media conglomerate with extensive sports broadcasting capabilities, brings substantial resources and distribution channels to the partnership. The network's involvement signals confidence in the PWHL's product and its potential to capture audiences across multiple demographics and geographic regions throughout the United States.

## Understanding the Media Landscape

The women's sports media landscape has undergone dramatic transformation over the past several years. Increased investment in women's athletics, driven by growing fan engagement and improved production quality, has created unprecedented opportunities for leagues like the PWHL. Major media companies now recognize the commercial viability of women's sports content, leading to more favorable broadcast agreements and higher-profile television slots.

The PWHL entered this landscape at an opportune moment. With six franchises representing major markets—Montreal, Toronto, Ottawa, Boston, New York, and Minnesota—the league possesses built-in television markets and fan bases. The addition of Scripps Sports as a broadcast partner leverages these advantages and ensures that PWHL games reach millions of viewers nationally.

## What This Means for PWHL Fans

For dedicated PWHL supporters, the Scripps Sports agreement translates into improved accessibility and convenience. Games broadcast on national television networks reach broader audiences than regional broadcasts or streaming-only arrangements. Fans who previously relied on league-specific streaming services or regional broadcasts now have access to games through Scripps Sports' television networks and digital platforms.

The partnership also enhances the league's visibility among casual sports fans who may not actively seek out women's hockey content. Television broadcasts introduce PWHL hockey to viewers who might discover the sport through traditional channel surfing or digital recommendations. This passive discovery mechanism has historically been crucial for growing sports audiences and building new fan bases.

Additionally, the television agreement likely includes enhanced production values, better camera angles, improved commentary, and professional-grade presentation. These production improvements elevate the overall viewing experience and contribute to the sport's perception as a premiere professional league worthy of mainstream attention.

## Financial and Strategic Implications

From a financial perspective, television partnerships generate critical revenue streams for professional sports leagues. Broadcast rights fees provide guaranteed income that helps fund league operations, player salaries, and franchise investments. The Scripps Sports agreement contributes to the PWHL's financial sustainability and provides resources for continued expansion and improvement.

The partnership also represents validation of the PWHL's business model from a major media company's perspective. When established broadcasters commit to multi-year agreements, they're making significant financial bets based on detailed market analysis and viewership projections. This institutional confidence from Scripps Sports reinforces investor and stakeholder confidence in the league's long-term viability.

Moreover, the television agreement positions the PWHL competitively within the broader sports media ecosystem. As networks allocate premium broadcast slots and resources, the PWHL gains leverage in scheduling, promotional support, and audience development initiatives. These advantages compound over time, creating positive feedback loops that strengthen the league's market position.

## The 2026-27 Timeline

The agreement's effective date of the 2026-27 season provides the PWHL with valuable preparation time. The league can work with Scripps Sports on content strategy, broadcast scheduling, and promotional campaigns well in advance of the agreement's commencement. This runway allows both parties to optimize the partnership and ensure seamless integration of PWHL content into Scripps Sports' broadcasting schedule.

The timing also aligns with the PWHL's anticipated growth trajectory. By 2026-27, the league may have expanded its franchise footprint, developed additional rivalries, and built deeper fan bases in existing markets. These developments enhance the attractiveness of broadcast content and increase viewership potential for Scripps Sports.

## Building on Previous Broadcasting Success

The PWHL's inaugural season demonstrated strong television appeal. Games broadcast through existing arrangements generated solid ratings and engaged audiences across multiple markets. The league's young stars—emerging talents who are revolutionizing women's hockey—proved to be compelling television subjects. Player personalities, competitive intensity, and exciting gameplay all contributed to positive viewer reception.

Scripps Sports' investment builds on this foundation. Rather than establishing an untested property, the network is partnering with a league that has already demonstrated audience appeal and commercial viability. This de-risked approach reflects prudent media business strategy while supporting the PWHL's continued growth.

## Broader Impact on Women's Sports

The PWHL-Scripps Sports partnership extends beyond hockey, contributing to the broader momentum building in women's professional sports. Major broadcast agreements for women's leagues create precedent, normalize women's sports coverage, and encourage other broadcasters to invest in women's athletic content. Each successful partnership makes subsequent agreements more achievable for other leagues.

This ecosystem effect strengthens the entire women's sports sector. As television networks commit resources to women's hockey, basketball, soccer, and other sports, cultural perceptions shift. Young athletes see career pathways; sponsors see target audiences; fans see legitimacy and professionalism. The PWHL partnership thus represents progress not only for hockey but for women's athletics generally.

National television partnerships unlock substantial marketing and promotional opportunities. Scripps Sports brings advertising reach, promotional platforms, and audience development expertise. Cross-promotion with other Scripps Sports programming, strategic scheduling during premium time slots, and integrated promotional campaigns all become possible.
